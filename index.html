Footprint RCU4

People counting, proven worldwide

A people counter for single or multi-door use, with hour, day, week, month and year totals. Cable or wireless options available, easy to use and affordable.

In business today it's vital that decisions are based on informative data to increase efficiency and maximise profit. The Footprint RCU4 People Counter is designed to give you an easy-to-use tool that reports foot traffic on an hourly basis. Thanks to its impressive performance at a realistic price, coupled with outstanding reliability, it's the choice of modern retailers throughout the world.

An increasing number of retail companies rely on people counting to understand traffic flow through their stores. Match that data against actual sales and you can accurately track your sales conversion rate, and the gap between the two is how many sales you're missing out on. Lift your conversion rate and you lift your profit.

Using the latest microprocessor technology, the RCU4 can monitor up to four separate doors simultaneously, with separate counts per door and a combined total for multi-door sites. It runs completely independent of your computer, storing count data internally for up to one week, so there's no loss of data should your computer malfunction.

  • Monitor up to four doors at once, with per-door and combined totals
  • Hour, day, week, month and year count totals
  • Cable or wireless options, link multiple units for any number of doors
  • Stores up to a week of data on-board, no loss if your PC goes down

Who we are

Forty years of serious Kiwi electronics

Electronic Specialists is an Invercargill design and manufacturing company building telemetry and SCADA automation for the agricultural, industrial and retail sectors. We've been at it since 1985, with deep experience in serial-data communication and a range of monitoring, remote-control, data-logging and text-alert products.

Our Farmtrenz division builds the fail-safe TIM and KIM effluent field devices — a market leader for over ten years — while Footprint people counters have run in shops and museums worldwide for 20-plus years. If electronics can solve it, chances are we've built something for it, or we can.

  • NZ owned & operating since 1985 — designed and manufactured right here in Southland.
  • Fail-safe field-proven gear — the latest UHF telemetry and GSM networking, hardened for real conditions.
  • We service what we sell — with authorised installers available near you.
