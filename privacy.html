1. Introduction

Electronic Specialists ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect the personal information you share with us when you use our website or get in touch. We handle personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you give us when you call, email or enquire — such as your name, phone number, email address and business.
  • Details about your enquiry or project so we can quote and help you.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends when you visit the site (such as general location and pages viewed), used only to keep the site working well.

3. How we use it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, supply and service products, and keep you updated about work we're doing for you. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

We only share your information where it's needed to deliver a product or service — for example with an authorised installer working on your job — or where we're required to by law. Any third parties we use are expected to protect your information.

5. Storage & security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to hold it only as long as we need it. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect the details you share with us.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request a correction. To do so, contact us using the details below.

7. Cookies

This site may use basic cookies or similar technology to help it function and to understand general usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.
